Financing for your project

Monthly payments can make a bigger project possible. We work with Acorn Finance, which compares offers from a network of lenders so you can pick the one that fits your budget.

  • Projects from $1,000 to $100,000
  • Prequalify in minutes
  • Compare offers side by side
  • Apply from any phone or computer
  1. Apply onlineAnswer a few questions about you and the project. Most people finish in a few minutes.
  2. PrequalifyYou see which lenders will offer you a loan. Prequalifying uses a soft credit check.
  3. Compare offersLook at amounts, terms and payments side by side and choose the one you like, or none at all.
  4. Get funded and startAfter you accept an offer and the lender approves it, we schedule your project.
Apply for financingPrefer to do it together? Your estimator can walk you through financing during your free estimate, often with a decision the same day.
Financing is offered by independent lenders through Acorn Finance and is subject to credit review and approval. Summit Exterior Group is not a lender and does not make credit decisions. Rates, terms and funding times depend on your credit profile and the lender you choose. Prequalification uses a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score. A lender may perform a hard credit inquiry, which can affect your score, when you choose an offer and move forward. Not all applicants will qualify. Questions homeowners ask

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. We start with a free, no-pressure look at your project so you get honest information before deciding anything.

How long does the inspection take?

Most visits take about 60 to 90 minutes, depending on the size of the project and how many questions you have.

Will I be pressured to buy?

No. Sometimes the right answer is a full project, sometimes a repair, and sometimes it is that your home is okay for now. We explain your options and you decide.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We have payment plans that fit a wide range of situations, and your inspector will walk through the options with you.

What services do you offer?

Roofing, siding, windows, bathrooms, seasonal maintenance, fencing, and all types of interior work as a general contractor.

Where do you work?

We are based in Portland, Oregon. Call or send a request and we will confirm we can serve your address.
